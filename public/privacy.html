Privacy

What We Collect

When you sign in with Google, Vouch receives the basic account information provided by Google for sign-in, such as your name and email address. Vouch does not access your Gmail, Google Drive, or other Google services.

Your email address and name are used to create and identify your Vouch account. They are not shown publicly and are not used as your public identity on the site.

Your account may also store your nickname, date joined, selected Top 4, reviews, ratings, review dates, likes, accounts you follow, and notifications. If you submit an eatery suggestion, feedback, or a report, that message is stored with your account. You can edit or delete your own reviews.

What Others Can See

Your reviews are shown under the nickname you choose. Other users cannot see your Google name or email address.

Other users can see your nickname, reviews, ratings, review dates, likes received on your reviews, selected Top 4, any badges assigned to your account, and your follower and following counts. They cannot see your Google name, email address, which reviews you have liked, or who you follow.

Following, Notifications, and Messages

If you follow other users, your followers and following lists are visible only to you. Only the counts are shown on profiles.

Vouch may send you in-app notifications, such as when an eatery you suggested is added. Notifications are visible only to your account. We keep your most recent 50, and read notifications are deleted automatically after 30 days.

Eatery suggestions, feedback messages, and reports you submit are visible only to site administrators. Feedback may be grouped with similar requests, and we may reply to it on the feedback page.

Why We Require Sign-In

You can browse Vouch and read reviews without an account.

Google sign-in is required for actions that need an account, such as posting reviews and interacting with other users. This allows Vouch to associate reviews and other activity with an account while keeping your Google identity private from other users.

Moderation

Site administrators can technically see which account is associated with a review. This information is used for moderation and handling reports.

Site administrators may hide or delete reviews when necessary for moderation. Accounts may also be deleted when necessary. Account deletion is handled manually through the site's database.

Services We Use

Vouch uses Supabase for its database and authentication, Google OAuth for sign-in, and Vercel for hosting. The site also uses local storage in your browser for site functionality.

Deleting Your Account
